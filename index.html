    Collaborators wanted: open-source Yiddish TTS

    We are building an open Yiddish text-to-speech stack aimed at a NotebookLM-style Yiddish podcast generator (two natural voices discussing a document), plus Yiddish ASR to evaluate it. Most needed: Hasidic-accent speech data and speakers, G2P / loshn-koydesh pronunciation expertise, TTS model training (VibeVoice, F5, Chatterbox, Piper), and help setting up a public Yiddish ASR/TTS benchmark modelled on ivrit.ai's leaderboards.
